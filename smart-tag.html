The tag measures each throw from the moment you let go.

    How fast the disc leaves your hand. More speed can mean more distance.

    How fast the disc rotates. Spin helps a disc hold its line.

    The angle of the disc as you let go. It shapes the whole flight.

    How far the throw goes. The number everyone wants to grow.
